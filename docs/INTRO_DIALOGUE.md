# Intro dialogue (6 lines, skippable, shown once on a new game)

One short scene with a beginning, a middle and a goal. Every line is spoken **to** someone and answers the line before it.

The player is **Noon**, the shadow the dial casts at midday: a faceless silhouette, never given a face. The *Patience* has just broken on the reef; Noon has washed ashore on the beach, and Nell (who steered the ferry) is shaking Noon awake. Portraits match each character's in-game puppet look (Hesper: charcoal coat, terracotta sash, ledger; Tobias: field-green, fiddle; Ada: clockworks brown, goggles, gear; Nell: watch-grey oilskin, rope).

Portraits: public/portraits/{noon,nell,ada,tobias,hesper}.webp (384px WebP, keep each under 40 KB).
Speaker name plate + portrait left/right (Noon on the left, everyone else on the right), typewriter text, tap to advance, Skip always visible.
The camera starts on the shore, moves to Hesper's tent for her line, and pulls back to the whole island for the last one.

1. **Nell** (to Noon): "Noon! Wake up. The Patience broke on the reef. Six of us got ashore, and you."
2. **Noon** (to Nell): "Nell... where are we? Whose tent is that?"
3. **Hesper** (to Noon): "Mine. Welcome ashore, Noon. I am Hesper, the Clockkeeper. You have eight Hours between you, and I lend more."
4. **Tobias** (to Noon): "Careful, Noon. Every hour she lends comes due at dusk, with a little extra. We ran from Aster to get away from that."
5. **Ada** (to Noon): "And dusk brings the Late, rowing in from islands that ran out of time. They want Hours, not blood. Walls turn them back."
6. **Noon** (to everyone): "Then we build before dark. The Palisade first."

What it sets up, in order: what just happened (the wreck), where we are (Hesper's island), the debt (borrowed hours come due at dusk), the dusk raids (the Late), and the first goal (the Palisade). The Hesper card that follows ("We're starving. Borrow 10 Hours?") is her first offer, and the coach then points at the Palisade.
