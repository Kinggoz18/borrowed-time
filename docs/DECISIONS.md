| 27 | 2026-10-10 | **Landmarks are placed by the player; moving a building.** (a) The six cosmetic landmarks no longer appear on their own. Town unlocks three (Town Clock, Founders' Wreck, Hesper's First Bargain), City three more (Great Dial, Lighthouse, Tide-Bell). They are listed in a "Landmarks" section of the Build sheet (only the unlocked ones are in the DOM), are **free, one of each, and cosmetic**: no Hours, defence or housing. The player places each on a free lot or on a plaza tile (a street crossing in the built area, never on the avenue to the gate) and can move it for free any time it is day. Tap still shows the lore plaque. State: optional `landmarks` map (id → lot key or `p:I,J`); it is absent until the first placement, so the state hash of every game that never uses it is unchanged, and old saves (which stored none) simply load with all landmarks unplaced. A lot holding a landmark is not free (`isFree`, footprint claims). Commands `landmark` and `move` replay like any other. (b) **Move a building:** select a building, Move (key M with its sheet open), pick a lit free lot, confirm. (c) Toasts, coach tips and the spot picker are placed from measured insets (`--bar-top`, `--rail-w`) so they never cover the action bar in the phone rail, laptop bar or portrait strip (e2e asserts it at 1440x900, 1920x1080, 667x375@2x and 360x800).
