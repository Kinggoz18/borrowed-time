# Decisions log

Design ambiguities resolved during the build, with the option the plan or lore supports. Newest last.

| # | Date | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-10-09 | **Greybox skipped (owner).** Phase 1 renders the Colony and Village eras with procedural stand-in sprites in the art bible style and ships a phone-playable build. | Owner instruction. |
| 2 | 2026-10-09 | The rules port follows `sim-test.js` with `RULES=build` and its default env values (seal gate, 12-day lien, salvage 0.3, rebuild at half price, keep 0.25+0.2n, upgrade XP 1+stage, boats, Great Dial, tier levels 1/3/7/11, City raid scale ×1.12). | It is the only rule set the gates have been run on. |
| 3 | 2026-10-09 | Unsimulated §3 tuning items (surprise raid nights, jobs/posts/hunger timers, pity, catch-up, new-shore breather, first Long Dusk ×0.7, trust seals) are not in the Phase 1 rules unless listed below as landed. | Prove parity first; add each later behind the gates. |
| 4 | 2026-10-09 | Dusk shows the lore hint (kind + light/even/heavy band) and the defence of each choice, not the raiders' number. | Plan §3 "Raids are a surprise": the hint never gives a number. Presentation only, so the gates are unaffected. |
| 5 | 2026-10-09 | The pure rules live in `src/core`. The Phase 0 stress scene keeps `src/sim` (scene data) and stays reachable at `?stress=1`. | Avoids breaking the Phase 0 device test. |
| 6 | 2026-10-09 | **Phase 1 balance pass.** The first full gate run on the ported rules failed four gates (Colony win rate 97%, City 70%, leverage dominant in Village and Town). Changed three knobs only: Colony raid scale 1.00 → 1.06, City 1.12 → 1.20, and the Long Dusk's appetite for debt 0.55 → 0.80 with a per-tier multiplier ×1 / ×1.25 / ×1.75 / ×1.25. All gates pass after (docs/SIM_GATES.md). `useRuleset("prototype")` restores the baseline, so parity with the prototype is still tested bit for bit. Rules version `v2-build.2`. | Lore: the Long Dusk "feeds on what is owed" and a bigger ledger casts a longer shadow, so heavy borrowers pay at the season's end instead of winning everything; the first shore is not safe either. The no-dominant gate needs borrowing to trade safety for growth, which is the game's whole bet. |
| 7 | 2026-10-09 | UI is DOM over the Pixi canvas (HUD, sheets, cards); the island is Pixi. | Crisp text at any DPR, real buttons for accessibility and Playwright, zero draw calls for UI. |
| 8 | 2026-10-09 | The stand-in atlas is drawn procedurally at boot (one 2048² page per era and quality tier) instead of shipped PNGs. | Same atlas discipline (one page, shelf-packed, mipmapped) with no asset pipeline yet; Phase 2 swaps in painted PNGs behind the same frame names. Boot cost is measured in the e2e. |
| 9 | 2026-10-09 | Colony and Village have their own art columns; Town and City reuse the Village art until their era columns are drawn. | The Phase 1 arc ends at Village. |
| 10 | 2026-10-09 | One in-game hour = 2.5 s at 1× (about 30 s a day), 8× with Rest. The clock pauses while a sheet or card is open. | Plan §2 "a day is about half a minute"; pausing keeps decisions calm on a phone. |
| 11 | 2026-10-09 | Raid nights hint "skiffs" or "longboats" by a fixed pattern of season and day; the rules have one raid kind. | The hint table has two kinds; the sim does not. Presentation only, no RNG draw (tested). |
| 12 | 2026-10-09 | Dev-build debug grants change the island outside the command log, so they start a new replay checkpoint. | Keeps `verify()` true; the debug panel is absent from production builds. |
| 13 | 2026-10-09 | The charter sheet shows food and homes, because people who can't be fed or housed leave at dawn before the tier check. | Found in e2e: "45/36 people" looked met but the tier never came. Clarity over surprise. |
| 14 | 2026-10-09 | Rewarded video is the only ad format. No interstitials, so no "remove ads" purchase. | Owner. Phase 1 has no ad code; the plan's monetization section says rewarded-only. |
| 15 | 2026-10-09 | The game is landscape only: Android locks `sensorLandscape`, the web manifest says `landscape`, a browser held upright shows "Turn your phone sideways". HUD is one strip on top, actions are a rail on the right (thumb side), sheets slide in from the right, cards are centred with art on the left and buttons side by side. E2E runs at 800×360 and 960×540. | Owner. Earlier portrait layout (360×800, bottom bar, bottom sheets) is replaced. |
| 16 | 2026-10-09 | Performance and stress testing are paused: the stress scene e2e and the perf unit tests run only with `RUN_STRESS=1`; `apk:gate` and the code stay. | Owner, until he says resume. |
| 17 | 2026-10-09 | Play starts at 1.6× the "whole island fits" zoom, centred on the plaza; pinch or scroll out to see it all. Buildings draw at 1.3× their lot and each type carries a signature mark (Clockworks gear, Hourglass sign, hearth smoke, Trade Post teal pennant, Watchtower red pennant). Grey (debt) buildings keep half their colour so types still read on grey land. | Owner: island too small, buildings tiny and hard to tell apart on grey land. |
