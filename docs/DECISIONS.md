| 24 | 2026-10-10 | **Day length and speed:** the day is `BASE_DAY` = 12 hours (one named constant in `src/core/rules.ts`; 10 is a one-line change) and the game runs at `GAME_SPEED` = 0.7× of the old clock (`src/game/session.ts`): one hour is 3571 ms (was 2500), a day about 43 s. "Rest" is `FAST` = `BASE_DAY` times, so the button reads **Rest 12×** and still runs an hour in about 0.3 s; the label, the tests and the docs all derive from those constants. The day was already 12 hours in the rules (income is paid per `BASE_DAY` hours), so no cost or yield was scaled and every sim gate is untouched: the sim counts in-game hours, never real time. The "8" players saw was the Rest multiplier and the starting 8 Hours of currency (unchanged: that is money, not daylight). Amends #10. | Owner: 12 (or 10) hours a day, 0.7× speed, update every 8. |
