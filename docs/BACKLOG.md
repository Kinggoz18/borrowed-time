## Lore tease: the Portal
Owner decision (October 2026). Nothing here is implemented now; this is the lore frame to tease later, so the store ideas above fit the game's rules.

- **The idea.** The colony can unlock a **time machine, or portal** (final name and look open). It opens onto other ages. The Modern, Futuristic and Fantasy art packages and the hour packs are the things that come through it.
- **Art packs are ages.** Each pack is a different age the portal opens onto (for example a modern age, a futuristic age, a fantasy age). An elite art pack is a permanent look from that age: the island and its buildings keep that look for good. This fits "ages are what time does to the island" (`LORE.md`, "Why time moves on"): the island still ages by keeping time, and the portal lets the colony see and keep the look of an age that is not its own. It is a look, not a new rule set, and it changes no gameplay number.
- **Hour packs are time carried back.** An hour pack is time brought back through the portal from another age. It is not framed as buying a shortcut or "selling Hours". This is how the plan's "never sell Hours" rule (`FINAL_PLAN_BT.md` section 8) and the owner's hour packs are reconciled: the lore is that the colony fetches time, and the rules still protect the core decision.
- **Fairness (hard requirements, to be written into the store design before any build).** No pay-to-win. Players keep a fair chance without buying: every season stays beatable with no purchase, and nothing bought changes raid strength or the dusk. Hour packs must never repay debt, forgive debt or stop a seizure, must be small and capped per day and scaled to the tier, and must go through the sim gates (a new gate for non-play Hours, as Quest's proposal suggests) before they ship. Art packs are cosmetic only.
- **Where it is teased.** Later: a line in the lore, a bottle or a sail from another dial hinting at a door in the dial, and a locked "Portal" entry that does nothing yet. Open questions it must not answer: whose years the island overflows with (`LORE.md` open question 7), and whether two dials can share an age (question 8).
- **Not yet decided.** Whether the portal is a building, a Great Dial feature, or a gift from Hesper (and what that says about her); the price points; the backend and receipt validation the store needs.
